## 1. 📱 "Minimalist Phone" (com.qqlabs.minimalistlauncher)

### Monetization Model
- **Trial-to-Paid Architecture:** Does not maintain a permanent, viable free tier for new users. Instead, it forces users through an onboarding quiz leading to a **7-Day Free Trial** attached to recurring billing.
- **Pricing Tiers:**
  - **Monthly Subscription:** ~$2.99 – $4.99/mo (most expensive over time).
  - **Annual Subscription:** ~$19.99 – $29.99/yr (billed after 7-day trial).
  - **Lifetime Unlock:** ~$39.99 – $49.99 (one-time purchase).

### Free vs. Premium Feature Breakdown
| Feature Category | Free / Trial Tier | Premium (Full Version Unlocked) |
|---|---|---|
| **Favorite Apps on Home** | Hard limit of **4 apps** | **Unlimited** favorite text apps. |
| **Short-Form Video Blocker** | ❌ Locked / Trial only | ✅ Active blocking for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Facebook Stories, Snapchat. |
| **Blocking Schedules** | Limited to 1 basic schedule | ✅ Multiple custom recurring schedules (e.g. *Morning Focus*, *Deep Work*, *Sleep*). |
| **In-App Time Reminder** | Basic reminder nudge | ✅ **Floating countdown progress bar** overlay + forced app lockout + mindful extension delay. |
| **Mindful Launch Delay** | ❌ Locked | ✅ Configurable 15-second breathing pause before target apps open. |
| **Monochrome Mode** | ❌ Locked | ✅ Automatic per-app grayscale conversion via Daltonizer. |
| **Notification Filter** | ❌ Locked or strictly limited | ✅ Unlimited app notification suppression + batched digest inbox. |
| **Customization** | Default black & white font | ✅ Premium color palettes (OLED Black, Pastel, Forest), custom fonts, and app renaming. |
| **App Hiding** | Max 2–3 apps | ✅ Unlimited hidden apps. |

## 2. ⚡ Niagara Launcher (Peter Huber / Niagara Pro)

Niagara is the benchmark for modern, fluid one-handed launcher ergonomics. While Minimalist Phone is purely anti-dopamine text, Niagara is a minimal-productivity hybrid.

### Monetization Model
- Free base version with **Niagara Pro** (~$9.99/year or ~$29.99 lifetime).

### Niagara Pro Key Features
1. **Anycons & Icon Assistant:** Generates minimalist monochrome/sticker icons dynamically for every installed app, eliminating colorful badges without requiring custom icon packs.
2. **Pop-up Folders & Swipes:** Swiping right on any favorite opens a nested sub-folder or secondary shortcut (e.g., swiping on Phone opens WhatsApp dialer).
3. **Usage Breaker:** An integrated digital wellbeing tool that tracks daily launches, highlights mindless phone checking, and displays a gentle pause dialog.
4. **Interactive Widget Stacks:** Stacks up to 4 custom widgets (weather, agenda, battery, music controls) behind the clock to save home screen space.
5. **Direct Notification Actions:** Expand, preview, and quick-reply to notifications directly on the home screen without pulling down the status bar.
6. **Double-Tap to Sleep & Custom Fonts:** Full typography customizer with device lock integrations.

## 3. 🛡️ Before Launcher ("Go Minimal")

Before Launcher strips away notifications and clutter with an emphasis on mindful notification delivery.

### Monetization Model
- Free version with **Before Launcher Pro** (one-time purchase of ~$7.99, no recurring subscription).

### Before Launcher Pro Key Features
1. **Advanced Filtered Notification Rules:** Granular filtering rules allowing whitelist keywords (e.g., let "Urgent" or "Bank OTP" through while muting promotional pings).
2. **App Drawer Categorization:** Grouping apps into custom expandable drawers/folders (e.g., Work, Finance, Tools) instead of a single flat list.
3. **Pin to Top:** Pinned priority apps at the summit of the drawer.
4. **App Hiding & Bloatware Shield:** Removes pre-installed carrier bloatware and tempting apps from drawer view.
5. **App Renaming:** Change addictive names to intentional labels (e.g., change "Twitter" to "Wasted Time").

## 4. 🧘 Indistractable Launcher (Inspired by The Light Phone)

Heavily influenced by Nir Eyal’s *Indistractable* and the E-ink Light Phone II aesthetic.

### Monetization Model
- Free tier with **Indistractable Pro** (~$3.99 – $9.99 one-time).

### Pro Key Features
1. **Dynamic Light/Dark Mode:** Automatic theme toggling based on solar sunrise/sunset or system schedule.
2. **Integrated Minimal Tasks & Daily To-Dos:** A minimalist notepad and task list accessible via a single swipe from the home screen.
3. **Glanceable Screen Time Metric:** Displays a clean daily screen-time minute counter directly under the clock.
4. **Animated Minimal Weather Widget:** Plain-text temperature and weather conditions (`"Rainy, 22°C"`).
5. **Custom Font Import:** Load external `.ttf` / `.otf` minimalist fonts.

## 5. 🔓 Olauncher (Open-Source FOSS Model)

Created by Tanuj Notes, Olauncher is a beloved free and open-source (GPLv3) minimalist launcher.

### Monetization & Feature Philosophy
- **100% Free & Open Source:** Zero ads, zero trackers, zero internet permissions requested.
- **Features:**
  - 4 to 8 text favorites on home screen.
  - Daily automatic rotating minimalist wallpaper from Unsplash.
  - Double-tap to lock, swipe gestures.
  - Fast app search with auto-launch first match.
- **Pro / Companion Ecosystem:** Extended configurations and complex widgets are intentionally excluded to keep the core code pure and lightweight.

## 6. 🧠 Specialized Friction Apps: One Sec & Opal

While launchers control the home screen, dedicated friction apps like **One Sec** (psychology-driven delay) and **Opal / Freedom** (deep VPN-based blocking) specialize in behavioral psychology.

### Key Innovations We Can Adopt:
1. **One Sec (The 10-Second Breathing Pause):**
   - When tapping Instagram/TikTok, forces a full-screen breathing circle with haptic feedback: *"Take a deep breath... Do you still want to open Instagram?"*
   - Studies show this 10-second barrier reduces compulsive social media opens by **57%**.
2. **Opal / Freedom (Deep Work Lockouts):**
   - Unbreakable focus sessions where apps cannot be unblocked even if the user restarts their phone.
   - Whitelist mode: Blocks the entire phone except for a curated list of study/work utilities.
